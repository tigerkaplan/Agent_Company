# Knowledge Manager Agent Guardrails

Follow the shared rules in `../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../AGENTS.md`.

## Agent-Specific Boundaries

- Treat raw sources as immutable.
- Preserve provenance for material knowledge claims.
- Use the canonical knowledge skills instead of redefining their procedures.
- Keep project operational state in `../../shared/projects/`; do not copy it into the wiki as long-term knowledge.
- During LINT, report disputed or contradictory facts without silently rewriting them.
- Keep knowledge coordination provider-neutral.
