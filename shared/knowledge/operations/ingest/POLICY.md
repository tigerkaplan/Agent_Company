# INGEST Policy

- Follow `../../../../CONSTITUTION.md`, `../../../../AGENTS.md`, `../../README.md`, and `../../SCHEMA.md`.
- Preserve raw sources; corrections and new versions are separate raw items.
- Preserve provenance, access constraints, and known limitations.
- Prefer updating an existing relevant wiki page over creating a duplicate.
- Do not promote summaries, assumptions, or disputed claims to confirmed facts.
- Do not ingest current project TODOs, handoffs, blockers, risks, approvals, or workflow status as long-term knowledge.
- Do not ingest durable QUERY analysis automatically; run a separate INGEST decision when it is valuable.
- Keep changes Markdown-first, local-first, and provider-neutral.
- Do not use embeddings, vector databases, RAG, semantic search, or provider-specific knowledge adapters in Phase 3A.
