# LINT Checks

## Structure And Navigation

- Broken relative links.
- Orphan wiki pages not represented in `../../index.md`.
- Index entries that point to missing or moved pages.
- Pages stored in misleading categories.
- Missing useful cross-references between related pages.

## Knowledge Quality

- Contradictory claims within or across pages.
- Claims whose supporting source card is missing or unclear.
- Stale claims or evidence that needs review.
- Duplicate pages or substantially duplicated synthesis.
- Missing concepts or glossary entries needed to understand indexed pages.
- Explicit knowledge gaps and unanswered questions.

## Provenance And Boundaries

- Source cards without adequate provenance.
- Wiki claims that cannot be traced to source cards or labelled inference.
- Raw items that appear to have been overwritten instead of versioned.
- Operational project state duplicated from `shared/projects/`.
- Provider-specific policy leaking into canonical knowledge definitions.

LINT reports findings only. Corrections require a separate scoped operation or task.
