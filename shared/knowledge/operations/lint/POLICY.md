# LINT Policy

- Follow `../../../../CONSTITUTION.md`, `../../../../AGENTS.md`, `../../README.md`, and `../../SCHEMA.md`.
- LINT is read-only for raw material, source cards, wiki pages, and the index unless a separate correction task is explicitly approved.
- LINT may append its operation summary to `../../log.md`.
- Report contradictions and disputed facts; do not choose a winner without sufficient evidence.
- Report stale or weakly supported claims without inventing replacement facts.
- Do not convert missing knowledge into inferred knowledge.
- Keep operational project-state findings in `shared/projects/`, not in the wiki.
- Prefer deterministic link and structure checks when tooling is added in a later phase; Phase 3A defines no tooling.
