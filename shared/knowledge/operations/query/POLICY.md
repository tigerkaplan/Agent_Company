# QUERY Policy

- Follow `../../../../CONSTITUTION.md`, `../../../../AGENTS.md`, `../../README.md`, and `../../SCHEMA.md`.
- Use index-first retrieval and minimum sufficient context.
- Prefer synthesized wiki pages; descend to source cards or raw material only when needed.
- Do not treat wiki synthesis as stronger than its supporting evidence.
- Do not mix current operational project state into long-term knowledge retrieval unless the task explicitly needs both; keep their roles distinct in the answer.
- QUERY is non-destructive and does not update raw material, source cards, wiki pages, or the index.
- Durable QUERY analysis may be proposed for later INGEST but is not automatically promoted.
- Do not claim semantic search, RAG, embeddings, or vector retrieval in Phase 3A.
