# Skill: Report Builder

## Purpose

Combine approved internal data into a concise operational report for review, coordination, or decision support.

## Inputs

- Approved internal data, notes, metrics, or status updates.
- Reporting period, audience, and decision context.
- Required sections, format, or level of detail.
- Known risks, blockers, approvals, and unresolved questions.

## Outputs

- Concise operational report.
- Key metrics or status summary.
- Risks, blockers, dependencies, and open questions.
- Recommended next steps.
- Validation notes and missing data.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral.
- Do not create connectors, dashboard automation, runtime automation, or provider-specific copies.
- Do not invent status, approvals, metrics, or completed work.
- Do not store operational state, secrets, or raw connector data dumps.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the report purpose, period, audience, and approved inputs.
2. Organize data into the requested or most useful report structure.
3. Summarize status, metrics, notable changes, and exceptions.
4. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and missing data.
5. Highlight risks, blockers, dependencies, and approval needs.
6. Recommend next steps when the evidence supports them.
7. Produce a concise report suitable for internal review.

## Approval Boundaries

- Do not publish, file, send, or use the report for external commitments without applicable approval.
- Escalate reports involving financial, legal, account, personnel, safety, or reputational consequences.

## Completion Checks

- Report purpose, audience, and period are clear.
- Data is from approved inputs.
- Metrics, status, and approvals are not invented.
- Risks, blockers, and missing data are visible.
- External use remains gated by approval.
