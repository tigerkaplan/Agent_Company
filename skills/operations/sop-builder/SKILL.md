# Skill: SOP Builder

## Purpose

Convert a repeated process into a clear standard operating procedure while preserving human approval points and safety boundaries.

## Inputs

- Process description, observed steps, notes, or existing checklist.
- Process owner, audience, trigger, and desired outcome.
- Required tools, inputs, outputs, and constraints.
- Known approval points, safety boundaries, exceptions, and escalation rules.

## Outputs

- Draft SOP.
- Roles, triggers, steps, checks, and expected outputs.
- Approval points and escalation paths.
- Exceptions, safety boundaries, and unresolved questions.
- Review checklist.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral.
- Do not create connectors, workflow automation, runtime automation, or provider-specific copies.
- Do not remove human approval points or safety boundaries.
- Do not store operational state, secrets, or raw connector data dumps.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the process goal, owner, audience, and trigger.
2. Identify required inputs, tools, roles, and outputs.
3. Convert the repeated process into clear ordered steps.
4. Add checks, exceptions, approval points, and escalation paths.
5. Mark unclear steps, missing owners, and unresolved safety questions.
6. Keep the SOP draft reusable across businesses unless business-specific context is explicitly required.
7. Prepare the SOP for human review before adoption.

## Approval Boundaries

- Do not adopt, publish, automate, or enforce the SOP without applicable approval.
- Escalate destructive, external, financial, account, safety, legal, or personnel-related steps.

## Completion Checks

- The SOP has a clear trigger, owner, steps, checks, and outputs.
- Human approval points and safety boundaries are preserved.
- Exceptions and escalation paths are visible.
- Ambiguities and unresolved questions are marked.
- SOP remains a draft until approved.
