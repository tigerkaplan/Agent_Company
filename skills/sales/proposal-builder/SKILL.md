# Skill: Proposal Builder

## Purpose

Combine approved business context and client requirements into a clear proposal draft without sending or making commercial commitments.

## Inputs

- Approved business context, offer details, and constraints.
- Client requirements, goals, pain points, and success criteria.
- Pricing, scope, timeline, and terms only when approved.
- Relevant prior discussions, research, or qualification notes.
- Required proposal format or sections.

## Outputs

- Proposal draft.
- Assumptions and exclusions.
- Open questions and missing requirements.
- Review checklist.
- Approval needs before external use.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral.
- Do not create connectors, document automation, runtime automation, or provider-specific copies.
- Do not invent business capabilities, pricing, terms, proof, or client requirements.
- Do not store operational state, secrets, or raw connector data dumps.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the approved business context and client requirements.
2. Identify the proposal objective, audience, and required sections.
3. Map requirements to approved capabilities, deliverables, and constraints.
4. Draft the proposal with clear scope, value, assumptions, and next steps.
5. Mark unresolved questions, missing approvals, and unsupported claims.
6. Prepare a review checklist for commercial, operational, and accuracy review.
7. Keep the proposal in draft state until approved.

## Approval Boundaries

- Never send a proposal externally without applicable approval.
- Do not commit commercially, legally, financially, or operationally without approval.
- Escalate pricing, terms, guarantees, deadlines, legal language, and unusual risk.

## Completion Checks

- The draft uses approved business context and client requirements.
- Assumptions, exclusions, and open questions are visible.
- Unsupported claims are removed or labeled.
- Proposal remains a draft.
- Required approvals are named before external use.
