# Skill: Research

## Purpose

Collect, verify, compare, and synthesize evidence so decisions can rely on traceable sources rather than unsupported assumptions.

## Inputs

- Research question.
- Decision context and constraints.
- Preferred or required source types.
- Known sources, documents, repositories, or links.
- Freshness or jurisdiction requirements, if relevant.

## Outputs

- Source list with enough detail for review.
- Confirmed facts.
- Inferences.
- Uncertainty and unresolved conflicts.
- Comparison of viable options, when relevant.
- Concise synthesis and recommended next step, if requested.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources where possible.
- Verify source freshness when the topic may have changed.
- Do not invent source details or hide uncertainty.
- Do not collapse conflicting evidence into false certainty.

## Procedure

1. Restate the research question and decision context.
2. Identify the minimum source set needed.
3. Collect sources and note freshness, authority, and relevance.
4. Extract confirmed facts with traceable evidence.
5. Separate inference from confirmed fact.
6. Compare options against the stated constraints.
7. Identify uncertainty, conflicts, and missing evidence.
8. Synthesize findings concisely.

## Checks

- Important claims trace back to a source or explicit inference.
- Confirmed facts, inference, and uncertainty are labeled.
- Source freshness was considered where relevant.
- Options are compared against the actual constraints.
- Unresolved conflicts are preserved for decision-making.
