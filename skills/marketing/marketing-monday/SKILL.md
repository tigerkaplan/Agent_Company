# Skill: Marketing Monday

## Purpose

Produce a recurring marketing brief that summarizes performance, customer or audience signals, priorities, and recommended next steps.

## Inputs

- Approved campaign, content, audience, sales, or customer signal data.
- Reporting period and business priorities.
- Known experiments, launches, offers, or market events.
- Performance metrics and source notes, when available.
- Requested brief format or audience.

## Outputs

- Concise marketing brief.
- Performance summary.
- Audience or customer signal summary.
- Priority recommendations.
- Open questions, risks, and follow-up analysis needs.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral.
- Do not create connectors, runtime automation, publication workflows, or provider-specific copies.
- Do not infer performance or audience behavior beyond the supplied evidence.
- Do not store operational state, secrets, or raw connector data dumps.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the reporting period, audience, and approved data sources.
2. Summarize performance signals and notable changes.
3. Summarize audience, customer, reputation, or market signals.
4. Identify priorities, risks, blockers, and opportunities.
5. Separate facts, interpretation, and recommended actions.
6. List missing data and follow-up analysis needs.
7. Produce a brief suitable for internal review.

## Approval Boundaries

- Do not publish, schedule, or change marketing materials from the brief without approval.
- Escalate recommendations involving budget, public claims, pricing, legal risk, or reputation risk.

## Completion Checks

- Reporting period and source basis are clear.
- Performance, signals, and priorities are separated.
- Facts, inference, and uncertainty are labeled.
- Recommendations remain internal until approved.
- Missing data or stale evidence is visible.
