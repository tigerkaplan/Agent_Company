# Skill: Competitor Analysis

## Purpose

Compare competitors from evidence while distinguishing confirmed facts, inference, and uncertainty.

## Inputs

- Competitor names, market category, geography, and comparison goal.
- Approved source materials, links, notes, or research outputs.
- Comparison criteria such as offer, pricing, positioning, audience, channels, or proof.
- Freshness requirements and known constraints.

## Outputs

- Competitor comparison.
- Confirmed facts with source basis.
- Inferences and uncertainty.
- Opportunity, threat, and differentiation notes.
- Recommended follow-up research.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral.
- Do not create connectors, scraping automation, runtime automation, or provider-specific copies.
- Do not present stale, inferred, or unsourced claims as confirmed facts.
- Do not store operational state, secrets, or raw connector data dumps.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the comparison goal and criteria.
2. Identify the minimum evidence set needed for the comparison.
3. Extract facts from approved sources and note source freshness.
4. Compare competitors against the agreed criteria.
5. Separate confirmed facts from interpretation and uncertainty.
6. Identify gaps, conflicts, opportunities, and threats.
7. Summarize actionable findings and recommended next research.

## Approval Boundaries

- Do not publish comparative claims, ads, sales claims, or public materials without approval.
- Escalate legal, reputational, pricing, or substantiation risks.

## Completion Checks

- Comparison criteria are clear.
- Important claims are source-backed or labeled as inference.
- Freshness and uncertainty are considered.
- Public-use claims remain unapproved drafts.
- Follow-up research gaps are listed.
