# Skill: Content Calendar

## Purpose

Create a channel-level content calendar that turns approved goals and source material into an internal publishing plan.

## Inputs

- Planning period, supplied channel context, goals, audiences, themes, and available assets.
- Cadence, format, timing, campaign, and capacity constraints.
- Approved prior performance or community signals, when available.

## Outputs

- Calendar entries with date or sequence, channel context, format, topic, objective, source asset, and draft status.
- Coverage, dependency, uncertainty, and review notes.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not invent performance evidence, create connectors, or schedule or publish content.
- Keep overall strategy, positioning, and campaign ownership with Marketing.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the period, channel context, goals, cadence, and constraints.
2. Inventory approved themes, assets, events, and evidence.
3. Assign a purpose and suitable format to each proposed item.
4. Balance themes, formats, timing, dependencies, and available capacity.
5. Mark missing inputs, risks, review needs, and draft status.
6. Produce the calendar for internal review.

## Approval Boundaries

- Calendar creation is an internal draft operation.
- Publishing, scheduling through an external service, editing live content, paid changes, and account changes require applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- Period, channel context, cadence, and goals are explicit.
- Every entry has a purpose, format, source basis, and status.
- Gaps and dependencies are visible.
- No external action or publication is claimed.
