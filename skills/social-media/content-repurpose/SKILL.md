# Skill: Content Repurpose

## Purpose

Transform approved source material into distinct channel-level social drafts while preserving meaning, attribution, and factual boundaries.

## Inputs

- Approved source content and provenance.
- Target channel or format contexts, audiences, objectives, tone, and length constraints.
- Required calls to action, links, credits, and prohibited claims.

## Outputs

- Separate draft variants for each supplied target context.
- Source-to-draft traceability, adaptation notes, and missing-asset or review needs.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Preserve the source meaning; do not invent facts, quotes, results, endorsements, or permissions.
- Do not create connectors or publish, schedule, edit, or delete content.

## Procedure

1. Confirm source provenance, permitted use, target contexts, audiences, and objectives.
2. Extract the supported ideas, facts, and reusable assets.
3. Select an appropriate angle and structure for each supplied target context.
4. Draft distinct variants rather than mechanically truncating one version.
5. Check traceability, attribution, factual fidelity, and format constraints.
6. Flag permissions, assets, or review still required.

## Approval Boundaries

- Repurposing into drafts is an internal operation.
- External publication, live edits, public replies, and account changes require applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- Every variant maps to the approved source and supplied target context.
- Meaning, attribution, and factual claims are preserved.
- Missing permissions or assets are visible and no external action is claimed.
