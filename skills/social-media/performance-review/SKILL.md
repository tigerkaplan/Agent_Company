# Skill: Performance Review

## Purpose

Review supplied channel or content performance data and produce evidence-backed findings and next-test recommendations.

## Inputs

- Content item or channel scope, reporting period, objective, and supplied metrics.
- Metric definitions, comparison baseline, prior tests, and known data limitations.
- Supplied channel or format context.

## Outputs

- Performance summary tied to the stated objective.
- Metric changes, possible explanations, uncertainty, and data-quality notes.
- Prioritized next-test recommendations.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not invent metrics, causal claims, benchmarks, or attribution.
- Do not create connectors, alter live content, modify paid campaigns, or spend budget.

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope, period, objective, metric definitions, and comparison basis.
2. Validate that supplied metrics are comparable and note gaps or anomalies.
3. Calculate or summarize relevant changes without overstating precision.
4. Separate observed performance from possible explanations.
5. Recommend bounded next tests tied to evidence and controllable variables.
6. Flag recommendations involving strategy, budget, live changes, or approval.

## Approval Boundaries

- Performance analysis and test recommendations are internal operations.
- Live content changes, paid campaign changes, budget spend, and account changes require applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- Scope, period, objective, metrics, and baseline are explicit.
- Calculations are traceable and causal uncertainty is visible.
- Recommendations are evidence-linked and no external action is claimed.
