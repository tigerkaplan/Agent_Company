# Skill: Hook Generator

## Purpose

Generate opening ideas for supplied social content without overstating claims or inventing evidence.

## Inputs

- Topic, content premise, audience, desired action, and supplied channel or format context.
- Approved facts, proof, tone, constraints, and prohibited claims.
- Requested number or range of hook styles.

## Outputs

- Distinct hook drafts with optional style or use notes.
- Claim-risk, repetition, and review notes.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not use fabricated urgency, guarantees, unsupported superlatives, or misleading curiosity gaps.
- Do not create connectors or publish content.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the topic, audience, objective, evidence, tone, and requested quantity.
2. Identify defensible angles grounded in the supplied material.
3. Generate meaningfully distinct openings rather than superficial rewrites.
4. Remove duplicates, unsupported implications, and misleading claims.
5. Note any hook that needs substantiation or sensitive review.
6. Return internal draft options.

## Approval Boundaries

- Hook generation is an internal draft operation.
- Publication or incorporation into live content requires applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- The requested number of distinct hooks is present.
- Hooks are grounded in supplied context and respect tone and claim limits.
- Risky wording is flagged and no publication is claimed.
