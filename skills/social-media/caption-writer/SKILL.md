# Skill: Caption Writer

## Purpose

Draft channel-appropriate caption options from supplied content, facts, tone, and objectives.

## Inputs

- Content brief or source asset description.
- Supplied channel context, audience, objective, tone, length, facts, and call to action.
- Required terms, prohibited claims, and accessibility context.

## Outputs

- One or more caption drafts.
- Optional short, standard, or alternate-tone variants when requested.
- Claim, source, accessibility, and review notes.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not fabricate claims, testimonials, results, links, offers, or performance.
- Do not create connectors or publish, edit, delete, or reply publicly.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the content, channel context, audience, objective, facts, and tone.
2. Identify the core message and any required or prohibited elements.
3. Draft a clear opening, useful body, and proportionate call to action.
4. Adapt length and structure to supplied constraints.
5. Check claims, readability, accessibility cues, and uncertainty.
6. Label the result as a draft for review.

## Approval Boundaries

- Caption drafting is an internal operation.
- Publishing, editing live content, or sending a public reply requires applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- Drafts match the supplied facts, objective, tone, and constraints.
- Unsupported claims and missing context are flagged.
- The output is labeled as a draft and no external action is claimed.
