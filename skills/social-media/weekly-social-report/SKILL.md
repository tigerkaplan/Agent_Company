# Skill: Weekly Social Report

## Purpose

Create an internal weekly report that combines supplied content activity, channel performance, community signals, learnings, and next actions.

## Inputs

- Reporting period, supplied channel context, objectives, content log, and metrics.
- Performance-review, comment-analysis, and competitor-monitor findings when available.
- Prior-period baseline, experiments, constraints, and known data gaps.

## Outputs

- Weekly activity and performance summary.
- Content, community, competitor, learning, risk, and next-action sections.
- Data-quality, uncertainty, ownership, and approval notes.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not invent activity, metrics, causal explanations, or completed actions.
- Do not create connectors, publish content, modify campaigns, spend budget, or alter accounts.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the period, audience, objectives, supplied sources, and comparison basis.
2. Summarize content activity and channel performance from supplied evidence.
3. Add community and competitor signals with their scope and uncertainty.
4. Separate facts, calculations, interpretation, and proposed actions.
5. Prioritize next steps with owners, dependencies, and approval needs when supplied.
6. Produce the report for internal review.

## Approval Boundaries

- Reporting and internal recommendations are draft operations.
- Publication, public replies, live content changes, paid campaign changes, budget spend, and account changes require applicable approval.

## Completion Checks

- Period, objectives, sources, metrics, and comparison basis are explicit.
- Facts, interpretation, and recommendations are separated.
- Gaps, risks, owners, and approval needs are visible.
- No external action is claimed.
