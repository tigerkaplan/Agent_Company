# Skill: Post Planner

## Purpose

Plan one channel-level social content item before copywriting, production, review, or publication.

## Inputs

- Supplied channel and format context.
- Objective, audience, topic, source material, call to action, and constraints.
- Relevant approved performance or campaign context.

## Outputs

- Post brief with objective, audience, core message, format, structure, asset needs, call to action, and success signal.
- Assumptions, risks, dependencies, and review notes.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not replace Marketing strategy or invent business claims, audience facts, or performance results.
- Do not create connectors, schedule, or publish content.

## Procedure

1. Confirm channel context, format, objective, audience, and source basis.
2. Identify the single core message and intended audience action.
3. Outline the opening, supporting points, asset treatment, and close.
4. Record production needs, constraints, and success signals.
5. Flag unsupported claims and required review.
6. Return an internal post brief.

## Approval Boundaries

- Planning and drafting are internal operations.
- Publication, live edits, deletion, public replies, paid changes, budget spend, and account changes require applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- The objective, audience, message, format, and source basis are clear.
- Asset needs, call to action, risks, and review status are visible.
- The plan does not claim execution or publication.
