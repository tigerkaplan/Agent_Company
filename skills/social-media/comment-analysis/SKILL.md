# Skill: Comment Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze supplied comments or community signals for recurring themes, questions, sentiment, risks, and response opportunities without replying publicly.

## Inputs

- Approved comments, messages, or community-signal excerpts.
- Supplied channel context, period, language, objective, and sampling notes.
- Escalation rules and relevant verified business facts.

## Outputs

- Theme groups, recurring questions, sentiment signals, and representative paraphrases.
- Risk, urgency, moderation, product, or content opportunity notes.
- Optional response themes or draft options when requested.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Protect personal data and do not infer sensitive traits, fabricate prevalence, or treat a partial sample as representative.
- Do not create connectors, moderate content, or send public replies.

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope, period, source, sampling limits, language, and objective.
2. Remove unnecessary personal detail from working summaries.
3. Group comments into evidence-backed themes, questions, and sentiment signals.
4. Distinguish counts or supplied measures from qualitative impressions.
5. Flag safety, legal, reputational, privacy, or escalation concerns.
6. Produce internal findings and clearly labeled draft response options if requested.

## Approval Boundaries

- Theme analysis and response drafting are internal operations.
- Public replies, moderation, deletion, hiding, reporting, or account actions require applicable approval and an implemented connector.

## Completion Checks

- Scope and sampling limitations are explicit.
- Themes are grounded in supplied evidence and uncertainty is visible.
- Sensitive cases are flagged and no public action is claimed.
