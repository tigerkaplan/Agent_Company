# Skill: Competitor Monitor

## Purpose

Analyze competitor social content patterns from approved evidence without turning channel observations into unsupported market-level conclusions.

## Inputs

- Competitor set, supplied channel context, period, comparison goal, and criteria.
- Approved posts, observations, exports, links, or research outputs with source dates.
- Freshness requirements and known evidence gaps.

## Outputs

- Channel-content pattern comparison.
- Confirmed observations, interpretations, uncertainty, and evidence gaps.
- Internal opportunities, watch items, and follow-up research needs.

## Constraints

- Follow `../../../CONSTITUTION.md` and `../../../AGENTS.md`.
- Stay provider-neutral and platform-neutral unless the task supplies platform context.
- Do not scrape, create connectors, fabricate reach or performance, or infer overall competitor strategy from channel evidence alone.
- Route broader market or competitor strategy analysis to Marketing.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the competitor set, channel context, period, criteria, and source freshness.
2. Record observable themes, formats, cadence, hooks, calls to action, and engagement signals.
3. Compare patterns consistently across the supplied evidence.
4. Separate confirmed observations from interpretations and unknowns.
5. Identify channel-level opportunities and risks without copying protected expression.
6. List evidence gaps and follow-up research.

## Approval Boundaries

- Competitor monitoring and internal analysis are draft operations.
- Publishing comparative claims or changing live content or campaigns requires applicable approval.

## Completion Checks

- Scope, period, criteria, sources, and freshness are explicit.
- Observations and inferences are separated.
- Market-level conclusions are not claimed from channel-only evidence.
- No scraping, connector action, or publication is claimed.
